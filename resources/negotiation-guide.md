# Guide: Negotiation

FDE negotiation isn't about winning — it's about arriving at a scope both sides can deliver and measure.

**What great looks like**
1. **Anchor on outcomes, not features** — open every scoping conversation by asking what business result the customer is trying to achieve, so the whole discussion stays tied to value rather than a wish list.
2. **Name the constraint explicitly** — when a request is unrealistic, say so with a reason ("that's a six-week build; we have two") and immediately offer a scoped alternative that fits the actual timeline.
3. **Co-create the success criteria** — before any work is committed, write down what "done" looks like in measurable terms and confirm the customer signs off on that definition.
4. **Handle scope creep at the boundary** — when new asks arrive mid-engagement, acknowledge them, log them, and frame the trade-off: "We can add that if we drop X, or we scope it for the next phase."
5. **Close every conversation with a written summary** — send a brief recap of what was agreed, what was deferred, and who owns the next action; it prevents revisionism and builds trust.

**Anti-patterns:** agreeing to everything to avoid conflict; leaving success criteria vague; letting scope creep accumulate silently; negotiating against yourself before the customer even pushes back; over-promising on timeline to close the deal.
